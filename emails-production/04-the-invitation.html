The first consultation

— About 45 minutes, at the studio in Gambrills or on a call — whichever suits you.
— We look at your house, your plat, and the problem you’re actually trying to solve.
— I tell you what your lot will allow — zoning, setbacks, and what the existing structure will and won’t carry.
— You get a straight answer on realistic budget range and timeline. Including the answer you may not want.
— You leave with a written outline of scope — yours to keep, whether or not you work with us.

And what it isn’t: a sales meeting. It is complimentary — no fee, no drawings produced, no obligation. If what you have in mind doesn’t make sense to build, I’ll tell you that in the first twenty minutes — which is a great deal cheaper for both of us than finding out somewhere in Step 3.
