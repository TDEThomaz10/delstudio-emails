Residential Addition

Catonsville, Maryland  ·  Entry vestibule, bedroom extension, four-season room

They didn’t need more house. They needed more light.

{{FirstName}}, I thought you might like to see what the process in my last email actually produces. This one is close to home — a brick rancher in Catonsville whose owners were out of room and out of daylight.

We were commissioned to design a new entry vestibule, extend an existing bedroom, modify a bathroom, and add an all-season room and breakfast area. The design uses an arched entry into the four-season room, where a cathedral ceiling opens up the views and pulls daylight deep into the back of the house.
